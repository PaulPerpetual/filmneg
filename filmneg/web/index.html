Background

Crop & straighten

Drag the corners or edges to fine-tune, inside the crop to move it. A press outside the crop pulls the nearest edge there; ⇧-drag draws a new crop. Drag outside the picture to rotate. Changing the crop leaves exposure and colour alone. Distortion: + straightens lines that bulge outwards (barrel), − lines that bend inwards (pincushion). The grid helps judge both.

Dust

Click a spot (or ↑/↓): it shows unrepaired. Return shows the repair, Return again keeps it and moves on; ⌫ leaves it alone and moves on. Your decisions teach the scores. If a repair isn't good, ⌥-click a clean area to pick a clone source, then paint over the spot ([ ] = brush size, Esc drops the source). Click empty picture to mark a missed spot. Hold \ for the unrepaired scan.

Input Output
Drag a triangle, or click where it should go. Pinch, scroll sideways or use the magnifier to look closer. While dragging black or white, red/blue on the image shows what clips (like Lightroom). Double-click a triangle to reset.

Curves

Drag up or down. Press right on the curve for a narrow change, further away from it for a broader one; the shaded bell shows what will move. The Light curve changes brightness only, never colour.
Zones
Pin points with Measure (P), then pick the zone each should sit in.

    Colour by tone

    Shifts colour at one brightness only, e.g. warmer highlights or greener shadows. Shift colour on the image edits these curves too.

    Points

      Measure (P) pins a point: click, or drag over an area. Grey, White, Black and Skin pin a sample Solve makes right. Greys at several brightnesses fix casts that change from shadows to highlights. Zones for points are set under Curves, Light.
