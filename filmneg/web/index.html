Straighten casts

Looks at the near-grey parts of the picture in each brightness band and bends the warmth and tint curves so that they are neutral in every band. With “keep overall colour” on, it only removes the part of the cast that changes between shadows and highlights, and leaves the overall warmth or coolness alone.

Base

Curves

Grab a curve and drag up or down. Grabbing right on the curve uses the width setting; grabbing further away from it pulls a broader range. The triangles at the bottom are the black and white points; the image shows what gets clipped while you drag them. Double-click resets the curve.

Samples

    Pick Grey, White, Black or Skin and click areas in the image. Several greys at different brightness let “Solve samples” fix casts that shift between shadows and highlights. ⇧-click a sample to remove it.
