Straighten casts

Looks at the near-grey parts of the picture in each brightness band and bends the warmth and tint curves so that they are neutral in every band. With “keep overall colour” on, it only removes the part of the cast that changes between shadows and highlights, and leaves the overall warmth or coolness alone.

Input Output
Drag the black, grey and white triangles under the histogram; the lower bar sets the output. Pinch or scroll over the histogram to zoom in, drag it to move around, double-click to see everything. While you drag the black or white point, the image shows what gets clipped: red for white, blue for black, fading in just before it clips. Double-click the triangles to reset.

Curves

Drag up or down. Press right on the curve for a narrow change, further away from it for a broader one; the shaded bell shows what will move.

Samples

    Pick Grey, White, Black or Skin, then click a point or click and drag to sample an area. Drag a sample to move it. Several greys at different brightness let “Solve samples” fix casts that shift between shadows and highlights. ⇧-click a sample to remove it.

    Measure tool (P): click a spot or drag an area.
