Background

Crop & straighten

Drag the corners or edges to fine-tune, inside the crop to move it. A press outside the crop pulls the nearest edge there; ⇧-drag draws a new crop. Drag outside the picture to rotate. Changing the crop leaves exposure and colour alone. Distortion: + straightens lines that bulge outwards (barrel), − lines that bend inwards (pincushion). The grid helps judge both.

Input Output
Drag a triangle, or click where it should go. Pinch, scroll sideways or use the magnifier to look closer. While dragging black or white, red/blue on the image shows what clips (like Lightroom). Double-click a triangle to reset.

Curves

Drag up or down. Press right on the curve for a narrow change, further away from it for a broader one; the shaded bell shows what will move.

Colour by tone

Shifts colour at one brightness only, e.g. warmer highlights or greener shadows. Shift colour on the image edits these curves too.

Samples

    Pick Grey, White, Black or Skin, then click a point or click and drag to sample an area. Drag a sample to move it. Several greys at different brightness let “Solve samples” fix casts that shift between shadows and highlights. Hover a sample to highlight it; click its red × (or press Delete) to remove it.
